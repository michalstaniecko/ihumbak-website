# Glossary

**App**: a product by iHumbak.website presented on the site (for example Water Tracker Hydration, CV Drawer). Each App has a short, stable slug.

**App page**: the page at `ihumbak.website/<app>/` that briefly describes one App and links to its App documents and store or download pages.
_Avoid_: landing page, subpage.

**App document**: a page about one App at `ihumbak.website/<app>/<document>/`, such as `help`, `privacy` or `terms`. An App has only the App documents it needs.
_Avoid_: subpage (ambiguous with App page).

**Portfolio**: the list of Apps on the home page, each with a one-line summary linking to its App page.
