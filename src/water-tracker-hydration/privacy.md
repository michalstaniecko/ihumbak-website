This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Water Tracker Hydration mobile app for iOS and Android ("App").

## In short

- No account is needed. Your water intake, goals and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive them.
- The App shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. These Google services process device identifiers and technical data.
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before it shows ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the App.

## 1. Who is responsible

The App is provided by iHumbak.website Michal Staniecko, Poland, VAT ID PL6462645510 ("we", "us"). We are the controller of personal data processed in connection with the App.
Contact: [michal.staniecko@gmail.com](mailto:michal.staniecko@gmail.com).

## 2. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in the App stays on your device:

- water intake history, glass size and daily goal,
- reminder settings and active hours,
- achievements, streaks and in-app notifications,
- app settings, such as language, theme and date format,
- automatic backups of this data from the last 7 days.

The App does not ask for your name, e-mail, age, weight, gender, location or health records, and it does not connect to Apple Health, Google Fit or Health Connect.

The home screen widgets read today's progress, goal and streak from storage that only the App and its widgets can access, on the same device.

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud backup or Android backup to your Google account) may include the App's data, depending on your device settings. That backup is handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

## 3. Export and import

You can export your data as a JSON backup or a CSV file of your water history. The App creates the file and opens your system's share sheet; where the file goes (Files, e-mail, a cloud drive) is your choice. Import works the same way, from a file you pick. We never receive these files.

## 4. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The App asks for permission to send notifications; you can turn them off in the App or in your system settings. The App does not use push notifications and does not collect a push token.

## 5. Third-party services

The App uses the following services provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and Google LLC elsewhere). They run inside the App and send data to Google, not to us.

### Google AdMob (ads)

The App shows a banner and, occasionally, a full-screen ad. To show and measure ads, AdMob processes device identifiers (such as the advertising ID on Android or the IDFA on iOS), your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions.

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland**: at start, the App shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform). Ads are loaded only after you have made a choice. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show limited or non-personalised ads, as your choices allow. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy Settings** in the App.
- **iOS**: the IDFA is available to the App only if you allow tracking under Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you don't, ads are not personalised with the IDFA. You can change this in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your iPhone.
- **Android**: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

More: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Google Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)

Analytics helps us understand how the App is used: for example, how many people use it, on which devices and app versions, and which features are used. Firebase Analytics collects an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and events from the App, for example reaching a streak milestone or viewing a trend in statistics. It is not linked to your name or e-mail, which the App doesn't have.

### Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

When the App crashes or an error occurs, Crashlytics sends a report with the error details, device model, operating system and app version, and an installation identifier. A report may include the values involved in the failed operation, such as a water amount or a goal setting, so that we can find the cause.

---

The App turns Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics on or off together with ads, based on the result of the consent step.

Google's privacy policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Firebase: [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 6. Legal bases (GDPR)

- Personalised ads and the related use of device identifiers and storage: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in the App; this doesn't affect processing before the withdrawal.
- Non-personalised ads, usage statistics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in funding the free App, understanding how it is used and fixing errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), together with your choices in the consent form where the law requires consent.
- Answering your e-mails: our legitimate interest in handling your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 7. Transfers outside the EEA

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and Google also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 8. How long data is kept

- Data on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes it, except for copies in your own backups or exported files.
- Data processed by Google: for the periods set in Google's policies and in the App's Firebase and AdMob settings.
- E-mails: as long as needed to handle your request.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Because we don't hold your App data and the data collected by Google isn't linked to your identity, we usually can't identify you from a request. You control your data directly: change your ad choices in **Settings → Privacy Settings**, reset your advertising ID, export or delete data in the App, or uninstall it. For anything else, write to us.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, [uodo.gov.pl](https://uodo.gov.pl)), or the authority in your country.

## 10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.
