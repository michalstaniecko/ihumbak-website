# CV Drawer

Mac app · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon & Intel

A free Mac app for creating CVs and exporting them to PDF, or to DOCX for editing in Word, Pages, LibreOffice or Google Docs. Your CVs, the clause library and your settings are saved on your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere.
